TRANSPORTATION

Getting Around Taniti

Taniti offers several ways to explore the island. Public transportation serves Taniti City, while private buses, taxis, rental cars, bicycles, and walking provide additional options for reaching attractions and other areas throughout the island.

Public Bus

Public buses serve Taniti City every day from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m.

Private Bus

Private buses provide transportation to areas outside Taniti City and serve the rest of the island.

Taxis

Taxis are available throughout Taniti City for visitors who prefer direct transportation.

Rental Cars

Rental cars are available from a local rental agency located near the airport.

Bikes

Bikes and helmets are available to rent from several vendors. Helmets are required by law.

Walking

Taniti City is fairly flat and walkable, and Merriton Landing is especially easy to explore on foot.

ARRIVING IN TANITI

Getting to the Island

Most visitors arrive in Taniti by air through the island's small airport, which currently accommodates small jets and propeller planes. Some visitors also arrive by cruise ship, which docks in Yellow Leaf Bay one night each week.
