Welcome to Taniti

Taniti is a small tropical island in the Pacific covering less than 500 square miles. Despite its size, the island features a diverse landscape of sandy and rocky beaches, lush tropical rainforests, a safe harbor, and a mountainous interior that includes a small active volcano.

Taniti is home to an indigenous population of approximately 20,000 people, with fishing and agriculture traditionally serving as the island’s primary industries. As tourism continues to grow, visitors can experience Taniti’s natural scenery, local culture, outdoor recreation, dining, and other attractions throughout the island.
