LODGING

Find Your Stay

Taniti offers a variety of overnight accommodations, ranging from an inexpensive hostel to a large four-star resort. Visitors can also choose from many small, family-owned hotels and a growing number of bed and breakfasts throughout the island. Whether you're planning a family vacation, looking for a more personal island stay, or traveling on a budget, Taniti provides several options to fit your trip.

Family-Owned Hotels

Taniti has many small, family-owned hotels that provide a comfortable option for visitors looking for a traditional island stay.

Bed & Breakfasts

The island has a growing number of bed and breakfasts for travelers who prefer smaller, more personal accommodations during their visit.

Four-Star Resort

Taniti has one large four-star resort for visitors looking for a more upscale island experience during their stay.

Hostel

Budget-conscious travelers can choose Taniti's inexpensive hostel as an affordable lodging option while exploring the island.

Regulated & Inspected

All lodging establishments in Taniti are strictly regulated and regularly inspected by the Tanitian government.
