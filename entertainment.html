Make the Most of Your Visit

Most visitors come to Taniti for its beaches, tropical rainforest, and active volcano, but the island also offers a variety of activities for families, adventure seekers, and visitors looking to experience local culture.

Beaches

Relax along Taniti's sandy beaches and explore the shoreline surrounding Yellow Leaf Bay.

Rainforest & Volcano

Hike through the tropical rainforest or visit Taniti's small active volcano for a memorable island adventure.

Adventure Activities

Try snorkeling, zip-lining through the rainforest, chartered fishing tours, or a helicopter ride.

Family Fun

Enjoy bowling, visit the arcade, see a movie, or spend the day exploring family-friendly island activities.

Culture & Sightseeing

Visit the local history museum, explore art galleries, or take a boat or bus tour around the island.

Dining & Nightlife

Experience local restaurants, pubs, a microbrewery, and Taniti's new dance club.

MERRITON LANDING

A Growing Entertainment District

Many of Taniti's recreational activities are located in Merriton Landing, a rapidly developing area on the north side of Yellow Leaf Bay.
