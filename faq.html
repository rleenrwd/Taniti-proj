Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful information to know before visiting Taniti.

Power outlets are 120 volts, the same as those used in the United States.

Alcohol cannot be served or sold between midnight and 9:00 a.m.

The drinking age is 18.

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. Very little English is spoken in rural areas, especially among older residents.

Taniti has one hospital and several clinics. The hospital has many multilingual employees.

Violent crime is very rare, but visitors should remain aware of pickpocketing and other petty crime.

Many tourist attractions and restaurants close on national holidays, so visitors should plan ahead.

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar. Many businesses also accept euros and yen, several banks provide currency exchange, and many businesses accept major credit cards.
