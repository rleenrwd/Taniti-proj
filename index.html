ABOUT TANITI

The Heart of Paradise

Taniti is a breathtaking island where natural beauty and warm hospitality come together. From crystal-clear waters to towering mountains, every corner of Taniti offers something extraordinary.

Pristine Beaches

Relax on beautiful white sand beaches around Yellow Leaf Bay.

Lush Nature

Explore rainforest trails, waterfalls, and Taniti's active volcano.

Warm Culture

Experience local traditions, native architecture, dining, and island hospitality.

TRANSPORTATION

Easy Ways to Explore Taniti

By Air

Most visitors arrive through Taniti's small airport.

By Car

Rental cars are available from a local agency near the airport.

By Bus

Public buses serve Taniti City from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m.

By Taxi

Taxis are available throughout Taniti City.

By Bike

Bikes and required helmets are available to rent.
